Privacy Policy — FrostLedger

Effective date: October 6, 2026

FrostLedger (“the app”) is developed by Ray Ha, an individual developer. Contact: rhantho@gmail.com

Summary

FrostLedger collects no personal data. The app has no account system, no server component, no cross-device sync, and makes no network requests. The app does not send your inventory to the developer. If you enable iCloud or computer device backups, iOS may include app data in those backups according to your system settings. Those system-managed copies are separate from FrostLedger sync, which the app does not provide.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or telemetry of any kind.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No account creation, sign-in, or user profiles.
  • No network requests — the app has no backend.
  • No third-party SDKs. Apple frameworks only.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, or health data.

Data stored on your device

Your freezer inventory is stored in Apple’s on-device SwiftData store: batch names, food categories, portion counts, the compartment you assigned, the date you froze a batch, an optional planning target date, an optional note, and an activity history of portions added, used, discarded, restored, or corrected.

FrostLedger itself does not transmit or sync this data. iOS may include app data in a device backup if you enable that feature; those copies are governed by your Apple account and device-backup settings, not by FrostLedger. Deleting the app removes its local app data, but does not remove copies already present in a device backup or in files you exported.

Notifications

Reminders are optional. If you allow them they are scheduled locally with Apple’s UserNotifications framework and delivered by your device. No notification content is sent to a server, because there is no server. The app remains fully usable if you decline.

Export

You can export a CSV of your inventory. The file is written to your device’s temporary directory and handed to the system share sheet, where you choose what happens to it. The app does not upload it and does not read it back. Sample records are excluded from the export.

Backup and restore

You can prepare a restorable JSON backup file in the app and choose where to share or save it through the system share sheet. FrostLedger does not upload the file. When you choose a backup to restore, the app reads it on your device and replaces the current freezer data with the contents of that backup after you confirm. Keep your backup somewhere other than the iPhone if you need it after deleting the app.

Food safety

Planning target dates are reminders that you set. FrostLedger does not assess food safety, does not compute a date on which food stops being good, and makes no health claim. It is not a medical or food-safety device.

Children’s privacy

The app does not ask for personal information or create user accounts. It does not transmit inventory to the developer. Device backups, if enabled, are managed by iOS and the settings on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the app description will note the update.
