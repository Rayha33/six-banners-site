Privacy Policy — BatchBunker

Effective date: October 8, 2026

BatchBunker is developed by Ray Ha. Privacy questions: rhantho@gmail.com.

Summary

BatchBunker stores the incident information you enter on your device. The app has no account, developer-operated server, or app sync. It does not send incident records to the developer. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK. The operating system may include app data in device backups according to your settings.

Information stored in the app

Records can include an incident type, appliance name, dates and times, notes, affected-food names and quantities, optional value estimates, observations and their source labels, and images you select through Apple's system photo picker. The app stores these records locally using Apple's on-device storage frameworks. Selected image data is stored with the related incident.

Collection and disclosure

The developer does not receive incident records or selected photos from the app. BatchBunker has no account system, analytics, advertising, third-party analytics SDK, or app backend. The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, or health information. When you choose Share incident report, BatchBunker prepares plain text and presents Apple's share sheet. The report contains the information and estimates you entered and the count of attached photos; it does not include the image files. You choose whether to share it and which destination to use. That destination's own privacy practices apply.

Backups

iOS may include app data in an iCloud or computer device backup according to your device and account settings. These are operating-system-managed backups, not BatchBunker sync, and the developer does not control them.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain in the app until you delete them or remove the app, subject to any separate device backups or files you chose to share. Deleting an incident in the app also deletes its related food entries, observations, and attached photos from the app's local store. Copies already included in device backups or saved through the share sheet may remain under the settings and choices for those services.

Children

BatchBunker is a general household record-keeping app. It does not ask users to create an account or transmit information to the developer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised effective date.
