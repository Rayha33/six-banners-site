BatchBunker — Support

A private, offline iPhone app for tracking freezer batches and portions.

Getting help

Email rhantho@gmail.com. Please include:

  • the app version (Settings → About);
  • your iOS version and iPhone model;
  • the screen where the problem happened;
  • the steps that reproduce it.

Please do not include private food notes unless necessary to explain the problem. There is no server, so the developer cannot inspect your freezer data—only what you choose to describe.

Where is my data?

Your working inventory is stored in BatchBunker’s on-device database. The app has no account, server, or cross-device sync. If you enable iCloud or computer device backups, iOS may include app data in those backups according to your system settings. To make a separate restore point, prepare a JSON backup file in Settings and keep it somewhere else, such as Files or iCloud Drive. CSV exports from Activity are for spreadsheets and cannot be restored. Backups and exported files may remain after you delete the app.

Does BatchBunker tell me if food is still safe?

No. Planning target dates are reminders you set. The app makes no food-safety determination.

Why did my reminder not arrive?

Reminders need notification permission. Settings inside the app shows the current status and links to the system settings page. The app stays fully usable without reminders.

What are the SAMPLE rows?

They are demo content with a visible SAMPLE badge. They are excluded from inventory metrics and CSV export, and you can remove them at any time.

How do I get my data out?

Open the Activity tab and choose export. The CSV goes to the system share sheet, so you decide where it lands.
