Six Banners

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Six Banners ("the Game") is a fully offline, single-player fantasy autobattler for iPhone. It is free, it contains no advertising and no in-app purchases, and it collects no data of any kind. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. No data collection

Six Banners does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, device identifiers, or gameplay analytics. There is no server to send anything to, and no account under which anything could be held.

2. Completely offline, single-player operation

  • Single-player only. Every match is played on your own device against an on-device AI opponent. There is no online play, no multiplayer, no leaderboard, no chat, and no way to contact another player through the Game.
  • No accounts or logins. The Game does not require or support user account creation.
  • No networking. The Game makes no outbound network requests — not at launch, not during a match, and not in the background — and contains no tracking SDKs, third-party analytics, or crash reporting services.
  • No purchases or advertising. The Game is free to download, contains no advertisements, no in-app purchases, and no subscription.
  • No permissions or identifiers. The Game requests no device permissions. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt, and does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.

3. Local on-device storage

All game progress, unlockable cosmetic items (emblems, Citadel finishes, unit trims, battlefield themes), gameplay settings, and match statistics are stored strictly on your local device container using standard platform storage APIs. Nothing is uploaded, backed up to us, or shared. Deleting the application removes all locally stored game data; because we never received a copy, it cannot be restored by us afterwards. Six Banners does not opt its container out of your iPhone's own iCloud or Finder device backup, so a device backup taken beforehand may still contain it, exactly as for any other app.

4. Apple privacy manifest

In compliance with Apple developer requirements, Six Banners includes an official privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declaring zero tracking (NSPrivacyTracking: false), zero collected data types (NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes: []), and zero tracking domains (NSPrivacyTrackingDomains: []). The only declared API access is the standard user-defaults category, used solely to store your own settings on your device.

6. Children and age rating

Six Banners is rated 13+ for frequent fantasy violence. Because the Game collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children. There is no chat, no user-generated content, and no field anywhere in the app that asks for a name, an email address, or an age.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Because the app performs no collection, we do not expect a material change without a corresponding change to the app itself.
