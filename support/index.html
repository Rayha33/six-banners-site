Support

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Six Banners is a fully offline, single-player fantasy autobattler for iPhone. Choose one of three banners — Iron Banner (Humans), Ember Banner (Orcs), or Verdant Accord (Elves) — place permanent production buildings, and let your waves march. Every match is played on your own device against an on-device AI opponent. The app is free, with no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no subscription.

What the app needs from you

Nothing. There is no account, no login, no network connection, no in-app purchase, and no advertising. The app requests no permissions and transmits nothing from your device. It works in airplane mode, underground, and on a phone that has never been online.

Where your data lives

On the device only. Settings, progress, cosmetic selections, and match records are stored in the app's local container and are removed when you delete the app. The app has no cloud save and no cross-device restore of its own — your progress does not follow you to another iPhone, and deleting the app deletes it. There is no in-app reset. Six Banners does not opt its container out of your iPhone's own iCloud or Finder device backup, so a device backup taken beforehand may still contain it, exactly as for any other app.

Six Banners collects no data of any kind. The full statement is in the Six Banners Privacy Policy.

Getting started

Choose a banner and begin a campaign. Tap a + cell in your deployment grid to open the build chooser, then pick a producer. Place at least two producers and let the wave timer run — your units form up and march automatically. You never give orders to an individual soldier; you decide what to build and where to build it. Columns in the grid shape frontage and which targets your companies can reach; rows shape how quickly they arrive and how well they are screened.

A fortified bunker stands forward of every Citadel and must be breached before the Citadel becomes a legal target. Fog of war hides producers, companies, and objective state you have not scouted; your own structures and moving companies reveal ground as they go.

Progress and rewards

Experience unlocks emblems, Citadel finishes, unit trims, and battlefield themes. These are cosmetic only — they never change combat power, income, or score. There is no currency you can buy.

Age rating

Six Banners is rated 13+ for frequent fantasy violence. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no gambling or simulated gambling, no loot boxes, and no unrestricted web access.

Accessibility

The game is played in portrait throughout and is designed to be readable at phone scale without relying on small text. If a control is hard to reach or an element is hard to read on your device, tell us which screen and which device you are using and we will look at it.

Known issues

None reported for this release. Any confirmed defect will be listed here with the release that fixes it.
